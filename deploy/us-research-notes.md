## Verified on official sources (17 Oct 2026)
- EEOC (eeoc.gov/time-limits-filing-charge): charge within 180 calendar days;
  300 if a state/local agency enforces a law on the same basis; age: 300 only
  if a STATE age law + state body; Equal Pay Act: no charge needed, 2 years
  (3 if willful); federal employees: EEO counselor within 45 days.
- EEOC Birmingham District Office: Ridge Park Place, 1130 22nd Street South,
  Suite 2000, Birmingham, AL 35205 (eeoc.gov/field-office/birmingham).
- USCIS (…/i-am-a-lawful-permanent-resident-of-5-years): 18+, LPR 5 years,
  physically present 30 months of 5 years, continuous residence 5 years,
  3 months in the state/USCIS district.
- EOIR (justice.gov/eoir/recognition-and-accreditation-program, upd. 29 Apr
  2026): non-attorney "Accredited Representatives" may represent before
  DHS/EOIR, only through Recognized Organizations (non-profit, tax-exempt).
- DOL (dol.gov/agencies/whd/minimum-wage/state, upd. 1 Jul 2026): Alabama
  "No state minimum wage law"; FLSA employers pay federal $7.25/hour.
- Alabama Dept of Revenue, Recordation Tax (revenue.alabama.gov/?p=56784,
  upd. 14 Aug 2023): deeds $.50 per $500 of value or fraction; mortgages
  $.15 per $100 of indebtedness or fraction.
- AOC Annual Report 2023 (alacourt.gov): circuit courts — felonies, larger
  civil cases, domestic relations; district courts — smaller civil cases,
  small claims, misdemeanors; probate courts — wills, estates, guardianship,
  partition, name changes; municipal courts — ordinance violations.
  (Dollar limits left out: report is from 2023.)
- Alabama State Bar: Find a Member
  https://members.alabar.org/Member_Portal/Member_Portal/Member-Search.aspx ;
  Disciplinary History https://www.alabar.org/office-of-general-counsel/disciplinary-history/ ;
  Lawyer Referral Service https://www.alabar.org/programs/lawyer-referral-service/
  (was temporarily unavailable from 24 Mar 2026) ; Volunteer Lawyers Program
  https://www.alabar.org/programs/volunteer-lawyers-program/ , (888) 857-8571 ;
  Justice4AL https://www.justice4al.com (run by the State Bar; court forms,
  find a lawyer, other languages).
- Courts: judicial.alabama.gov ; trial circuits http://www.alacourt.gov/JudicialCircuits.aspx ;
  small claims guide https://judicial.alabama.gov/library/smallclaims
